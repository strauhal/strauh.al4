It's when you have two people and one of them sincerely believes in an idea, but the other person starts off by ironically entertaining the idea. However the person ironically entertaining this idea entertains it so much to the point where they are unsure of if they are sincere about the idea (meta-irony).

These two people are locked in conversation with each other and they are both trying to suss the other out.
