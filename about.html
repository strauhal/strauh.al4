strauh.al is a archive/collection of artworks and images i have downloaded from the internet since 2015/2016. it is the closest thing to my consciousness that i can capture in html.

the name "strauh.al" comes from my last name and i originally used it as a link to quickly download python scripts. however, the site grew bigger and eventually there was too much data linked on this website to rename it into something more archival sounding.

if you're on a mobile device and you enjoy using this site, add it to your homescreen.

if you're on desktop and you *really* enjoy using this site, you can create a clone of it on your computer by going to github.com/strauhal/strauh.al3.1.

strauh.al is continually updated and maintained through donations. donations can be made through visiting strauh.al/donate.

if you have questions or comments about the site, email me at ernest@strauh.al.
